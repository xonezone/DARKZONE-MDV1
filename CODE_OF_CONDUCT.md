# Code of Conduct

We want Dark Zone MD to be a friendly place for everyone.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and kind.
- Accept feedback politely and give it politely.
- Help newcomers. Everyone starts somewhere.

## What is not okay
- Insults, harassment, hate or threats
- Spam or advertising unrelated to the project
- Sharing other people's private information
- Asking for or sharing tools to abuse, scam or harm people

## What happens
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.

Message the owner on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/923309333332).
